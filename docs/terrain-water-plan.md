# Terrain and Water Management Plan

## Known Terrain Pattern

The farm slopes downhill from the house and compound toward the sugarcane area.

The river is located on the lower side of the sugarcane plantation.

## Main Risks

- Soil erosion during heavy rainfall
- Fast surface runoff down the slope
- Loss of fertile topsoil
- Waterlogging in lower areas
- Damage to paths or crop rows
- Nutrients washing toward the river

## Initial Management Ideas

### Contour Farming
Where possible, crop rows should follow the contour of the slope rather than running directly downhill.

### Grass Strips
Grass strips can be placed across the slope to slow runoff and trap soil.

### Drainage Channels
Small controlled drainage channels may help direct excess rainwater safely.

### River Buffer Zone
A vegetated buffer should be maintained between cultivated land and the river.

### Water Harvesting
Future water tanks or storage systems could collect rainwater from the house roof.

### Irrigation Planning
The river may become a possible irrigation source, but water access, legality, pumping distance, elevation, and dry-season flow still need to be assessed.

## Future Measurements Needed

- Slope percentage
- Elevation difference from house to river
- River GPS coordinates
- River flow in wet and dry seasons
- Soil type
- Drainage condition
- Erosion-prone areas
