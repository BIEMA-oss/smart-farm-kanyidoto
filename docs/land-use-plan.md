# Land Use Plan

## Total Farm Size
3.5 acres

## Proposed Farm Zones

### Residential Zone
Area around the existing three-bedroom house.

### Sugarcane Zone
A dedicated section for sugarcane production.

### Maize and Beans Zone
A section for maize and beans, including possible intercropping or rotation.

### Groundnuts Zone
A section for groundnut production.

### Millet Zone
A section for millet production.

### Trees and Mango Zone
Existing trees and the mango tree should be retained where practical.

### Water and Irrigation Zone
Future location for water storage, irrigation equipment, or water distribution from the nearby river.

### Access Paths
Space should be reserved for movement between crop areas and access to the house.

### Future Fence
The farm currently has no fence. A perimeter fence may be added later.
