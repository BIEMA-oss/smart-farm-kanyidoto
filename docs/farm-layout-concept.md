# Smart Farm Layout Concept

## Total Farm Size
Approximately 3.5 acres

## Purpose

This is the first conceptual land allocation plan for the Smart Farm Kanyidoto project.

The final layout will be adjusted after the exact farm boundary, house location, slope, soil characteristics, road access, and water source locations are mapped.

## Proposed Land Allocation

| Zone | Approximate Area |
|---|---:|
| Sugarcane | 1.2 acres |
| Maize and Beans | 0.8 acres |
| Groundnuts | 0.4 acres |
| Millet | 0.3 acres |
| House and Compound | 0.3 acres |
| Trees and Mango Area | 0.2 acres |
| Water, Paths and Future Infrastructure | 0.3 acres |
| **Total** | **3.5 acres** |

## Zone 1 - House and Compound

Approximately 0.3 acres.

This area contains the existing three-bedroom house that still requires finishing.

Possible future uses:
- Household compound
- Small kitchen garden
- Water tank
- Tool storage
- Composting area

## Zone 2 - Sugarcane

Approximately 1.2 acres.

This is currently the largest proposed crop zone.

The exact location should later consider:
- Soil conditions
- Water availability
- Access for harvesting
- Distance from the house

## Zone 3 - Maize and Beans

Approximately 0.8 acres.

Maize and beans may be grown together or managed through crop rotation depending on the farming system used.

## Zone 4 - Groundnuts

Approximately 0.4 acres.

This zone may also contribute to crop rotation planning.

## Zone 5 - Millet

Approximately 0.3 acres.

Millet provides another crop option and may be useful in relatively drier sections of the farm.

## Zone 6 - Trees and Mango Area

Approximately 0.2 acres.

Existing useful trees should be retained where practical.

The existing mango tree should be mapped later using GPS.

## Zone 7 - Water, Paths and Infrastructure

Approximately 0.3 acres.

This space may include:

- Farm access paths
- Water storage
- Irrigation equipment
- Future fencing access
- Drainage
- Utility areas

## Future Mapping Requirements

Before creating the final farm layout, collect:

- Exact farm boundary
- House GPS position
- River GPS position
- Mango tree position
- Main road/access position
- Land slope information
- Soil information
